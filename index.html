Credit skills

The steps I follow to judge repayment capacity

Each loan file moves through the same checks, from documents to a view on whether the borrower can carry the EMI.

Step 1

KYC verification

Identity and address documents collected and checked before anything else.

Step 2

Bank statement analysis

Income credits, spending and running obligations read from the statements.

Step 3

CIBIL analysis

Credit score and repayment history reviewed for past behaviour with loans.

Step 4

Financial statement analysis

Income and financial records studied for stability and size.

Step 5

FOIR

Fixed obligations measured against income to see how much room is left for a new EMI.

Step 6

Creditworthiness assessment

The checks above brought together into one view of repayment capacity.

FOIR formula (Existing EMIs + Proposed EMI) / Net monthly income
